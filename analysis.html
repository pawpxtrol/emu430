Key takeaways

  • Topic: Street-animal feeding operations across Ankara districts (ilçeler) from July 2024 to June 2025, based on monthly health-directorate operational records.
  • Data: ~3,700+ neighborhood-day records aggregated from 11 monthly workbooks, with feeding-point counts, kg/day of dry food, and vehicle / personnel staffing.
  • Important aspects: District names needed trimming; food / dates / staff fields were text-encoded with inconsistent formats (March 2025 dates as "1.03.2025", vehicles as "-" or the typo "Y3").
  • Interesting results: A handful of districts (notably Çankaya, Keçiören, Mamak) dominate row counts; monthly totals fluctuate, and the kg-per-record distribution is right-skewed with consolidated large drops in the tail.
  • Main outcome: A reproducible Quarto + R pipeline plus a publicly downloadable cleaned .RData enabling year-scale district comparisons and discussion of resource concentration and data quality in municipal animal-welfare operations.

feeding |>

    missing_mama       = sum(is.na(mama_kg)),
    missing_arac       = sum(is.na(arac_sayisi)),
    missing_personel   = sum(is.na(personel_sayisi))
  ) |>
  knitr::kable(caption = "Dataset coverage and missingness across all months")

The monthly workbooks do not always report every calendar day, so distinct_days is well below the calendar maximum. We treat that as a reporting feature rather than imputing days.

One row in the October 2024 workbook carries an obvious 2022-10-18 date (a source typo). It stays in feeding for traceability and shows up in the coverage table above, but we exclude it from the time-axis charts below so the monthly axis spans only the actual reporting window (Jul 2024 → Jun 2025).

Monthly volume

How many records per month, and how much dry food (kg) was reported per month?

Code 
monthly <- feeding_ts |>
  filter(!is.na(tarih)) |>
  mutate(month_start = lubridate::floor_date(tarih, "month")) |>
  group_by(month_start) |>
  summarise(
    rows = n(),
    total_kg = sum(mama_kg, na.rm = TRUE),
    .groups = "drop"
  ) |>
  arrange(month_start)

p1 <- ggplot(monthly, aes(month_start, rows)) +
  geom_col(fill = "#2E86AB") +
  scale_x_date(date_labels = "%b %Y", date_breaks = "1 month") +
  labs(x = NULL, y = "Records per month") +
  theme_minimal(base_size = 12) +
  theme(axis.text.x = element_text(angle = 35, hjust = 1))

p2 <- ggplot(monthly, aes(month_start, total_kg)) +
  geom_col(fill = "#A23B72") +
  scale_x_date(date_labels = "%b %Y", date_breaks = "1 month") +
  scale_y_continuous(labels = label_number(suffix = " kg", big.mark = ",")) +
  labs(x = NULL, y = "Reported dry food (kg / month)") +
  theme_minimal(base_size = 12) +
  theme(axis.text.x = element_text(angle = 35, hjust = 1))

Conclusions

  • Spatial concentration: A handful of districts (Çankaya, Keçiören, Mamak and a few others) account for most of the reported activity. Tying this to population, urban form, or program emphasis remains a hypothesis without external data.
  • Temporal patterns: Monthly volume is uneven, partly driven by reporting effort rather than real demand. The kg totals are less sensitive to that and offer a more reliable workload proxy.
  • Right-skewed food amounts: A long upper tail represents consolidated bulk feedings at specific points; the median is a better summary than the mean.
  • Operational fields: Vehicle/personnel are too coarse to claim per-day precision — interpret as background fleet/staffing context.
  • Data quality matters: Different months use different date formats, and one row carries an obvious 2022 typo — a reminder that reproducible cleaning is part of the analysis, not a side concern.

## Key takeaways

- **Topic:** **Street-animal feeding** operations across **Ankara districts (`ilçeler`)** from **July 2024 to June 2025**, based on monthly health-directorate operational records.

- **Operational fields:** Vehicle/personnel are too coarse to claim per-day precision — interpret as background fleet/staffing context.
